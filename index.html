Nond It privacy policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026
Developer/data controller: XtremeX
Privacy contact: ac8806605588@gmail.com

Nond It stores notes, checklists, categories, photos, reminders and preferences on your Android device. Notebook text and attached photos are encrypted locally. The app does not require an account or upload notebook content to the developer. App lock uses Android's biometric or device-credential services; Nond It does not receive your biometric templates or device PIN.

Photos, notifications and backups

Photos are selected using Android's system picker and copied into app-private storage. Android notifications deliver reminders; notification permission is optional. You can remove attached photos and notes in the app. Trashed notes remain until permanently deleted or removed by the optional automatic trash cleanup.

Manual exports and optional automatic backups write readable, unencrypted files to the destination you choose. If you choose a cloud-backed folder or share content through another app, that provider handles the copy under its own privacy policy. You must delete exported copies separately. Android automatic backup and device transfer of the encrypted notebook are disabled.

Advertising and consent

Nond It uses Google AdMob to display banner ads only in populated Trash screens. Notebook content, photos and search terms are not included in the app's ad requests. Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and provide its services. This data is transmitted to Google and processed under Google's policies. Advertising partners may process data as disclosed in the consent message and Google's policies.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy messages and manages consent or opt-out choices. You can reopen Advertising privacy choices in Settings when that option is applicable. Ad availability and personalization depend on your choices and applicable requirements.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Retention, deletion and your choices

Notebook content stays locally until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not remove files you exported elsewhere. Google determines the retention of advertising data under its policies; contact Google or use its privacy controls for that data. The developer cannot access your local notebook to recover or delete it remotely.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or withdraw consent. Use the in-app controls for local content and advertising privacy choices, and contact ac8806605588@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Audience and policy changes

Nond It is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to the developer, please contact ac8806605588@gmail.com. Do not send notebook content or other sensitive information when contacting us. Age requirements and parental-consent rules may differ by region; an intended audience of 13 and older does not mean every user can independently consent to advertising data processing.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date above.
